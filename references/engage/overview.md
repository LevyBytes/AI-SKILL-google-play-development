Integrate **Engage SDK** to publish personalized recommendations and continuation content to Google surfaces including [Collections](<https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/engage>), [Entertainment Space](<https://blog.google/products/android/entertainment-space/>) and the Play Store. The guide describes an average compressed APK addition of less than 50 KB and an integration estimate of about a developer week; assess the actual effect for your app. See the [Engage program](<http://play.google.com/console/about/programs/EngageSDK>) for business participation information.

> **Note:** **Get started:** Review the **[Eligibility, reach, and requirements](<https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/engage/eligibility>)** guide and [submit the Engage SDK interest form](<https://support.google.com/googleplay/contact/Engage_SDK>).
